Your Privacy Matters

Last Updated: June 30, 2026

1. Introduction

At Adinexis, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information including:

  • Name and company information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Business requirements submitted through contact forms
  • Browser, device, and IP address information
  • Website usage analytics and cookies

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to project inquiries.
  • Deliver requested services.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Communicate important updates.
  • Maintain website security.

4. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, analyze traffic, and enhance user experience. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not function properly.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services including analytics providers, cloud infrastructure, payment gateways, and communication tools. These providers maintain their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

Adinexis implements industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide services, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

8. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for communications.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes become effective immediately after publication on this page.
